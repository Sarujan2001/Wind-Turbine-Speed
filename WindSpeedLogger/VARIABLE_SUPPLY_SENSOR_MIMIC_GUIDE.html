Variable Supply Sensor Mimic Guide

SPL Wind Speed Logger | XIAO ESP32-C3 | SEN0170 0-5 V simulation
Bench test procedure
24 August 2026
Critical: The simulated 0-5 V sensor signal goes to J2 Pin 3 (WIND_RAW). Do not apply it to J2 Pin 1. J2 Pin 1 is the fixed +12 V sensor-power rail. Never apply 12 V to WIND_RAW, WIND_ADC, or the ESP32.

Confirm physical pin numbering from the PCB silkscreen or design before connecting power.

2. Initial wiring - ESP32 disconnected

Turn off all supply outputs before making or changing connections.

12 V supply + --------------------> J1 Pin 1 (+12 V) 12 V supply - --------------------> J1 Pin 2 (GND) Variable supply + ----------------> J2 Pin 3 (WIND_RAW) Variable supply - ----------------> J2 Pin 2 (GND) J2 Pin 1 = +12 V sensor rail; leave unused during signal simulation J2 Pin 4 = NC; leave disconnected
The 20 kOhm/10 kOhm signal divider is passive, so 12 V is not required merely to test the divider. The 12 V supply may be connected when checking the complete PCB, but it does not generate the simulated signal.

3. Configure the variable supply

  1. Keep the output switched OFF.
  2. Set voltage to 0.00 V.
  3. Set the current limit to approximately 10-20 mA.
  4. Verify positive is connected to J2 Pin 3.
  5. Verify negative is connected to J2 Pin 2/GND.
  6. Do not set the simulated signal above 5.00 V.

The divider normally draws only about 0.17 mA at a 5 V input.

4. Check WIND_RAW and WIND_ADC

  1. Set the multimeter to DC volts.
  2. Connect the black probe to J2 Pin 2/GND.
  3. Connect the red probe to J2 Pin 3/WIND_RAW.
  4. Switch on the variable output at 0.00 V, then set 0.50 V.
  5. Confirm WIND_RAW is approximately 0.50 V.
  6. Move only the red probe to the WIND_ADC test point or D1/GPIO3 header pad.
  7. Confirm WIND_ADC is approximately WIND_RAW divided by three.
Pass condition: WIND_ADC = WIND_RAW / 3, allowing for normal resistor and meter tolerance.

6. Connect and power the ESP32

Proceed only after the divider readings pass.

  1. Switch off all supply outputs and return the variable supply to 0.00 V.
  2. Connect PCB WIND_ADC to XIAO D1/GPIO3.
  3. Connect XIAO GND to PCB GND.
  4. Power the XIAO from USB, or from a properly regulated supply connected to its designated 5 V input.
  5. Avoid simultaneously powering it from USB and an external 5 V source unless safe isolation is confirmed.

7. Firmware verification

Open the serial monitor at 115200 baud. Increase the simulated signal gradually.

Example: 2.00 V at J2 Pin 3

WIND_ADC=0.667 V | SENSOR=2.000 V | WIND=12.00 m/s | 43.2 km/h

Example: 5.00 V at J2 Pin 3

WIND_ADC=1.667 V | SENSOR=5.000 V | WIND=30.00 m/s | 108.0 km/h

Small differences between the multimeter and ESP32 readings are normal.

8. Stop conditions and safety

  • Stop if WIND_ADC is not approximately one-third of WIND_RAW.
  • Stop if WIND_ADC exceeds approximately 1.7 V with a maximum 5 V input.
  • Stop if a supply enters current limiting or any component becomes warm.
  • Never use current/A mode with the meter connected directly across a supply.
  • Perform resistance and continuity tests only while all power is disconnected.
  • Never apply 12 V to J2 Pin 3, WIND_ADC, D1/GPIO3, or the XIAO 5 V pin.
